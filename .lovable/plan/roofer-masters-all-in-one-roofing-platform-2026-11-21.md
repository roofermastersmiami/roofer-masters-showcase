# Roofer Masters — all-in-one roofing platform

## Assessment and sequence

Preserve the existing website, accounts, records and payment safeguards. Both uploaded briefs define the scope.

**Present in reviewed source:** owner login, forms/chat, leads/contacts, private proposal templates, PDF annotation/form/page tools, signatures, basic estimates/materials/AI import, jobs/photos/change orders, invoices/manual payments/Stripe, follow-ups, sales, site editing and customer portals. These were not newly reverified during planning.

**Upgrade:** navigation, customer/property links, pipeline, estimating, documents, production, billing, portal and financial reporting.

**Build:** 3D editor, dashboard/search/notifications, multi-property customers, team permissions, calendar, inventory/suppliers, expense ledger, files and automations.

**Blockers:** payment-account setup, verified email domain, authorized SMS/calling provider. roofermastersmiami.com did not resolve here; outside-site integration is unverified.

Test each phase before the next. Preserve usable existing screens; no disconnected demos, fake transactions or dead controls.

### Phase 0 — Premium dashboard and CRM foundation

- Branded navy/silver/white/light-gray light/dark interface, collapsible sidebar, mobile navigation, search and notification center. Retain existing fonts and public website.
- Actual-record dashboard and requested revenue/conversion/approval/profit/balance charts, with Today/Week/Month/Year/Custom filters. Unavailable metrics are identified, never fabricated.
- Shared customers with multiple properties, billing/contact details, customer type/source, communications, private notes, documents and transaction/project history; preserve historical lead links. Keep street addresses off public pages; authorized property records/documents can contain property details.
- Requested twelve-stage drag/drop pipeline, retaining Lost and history. Card moves cannot manufacture signatures or payments. Add call notes, follow-ups and genuine email/activity history.

### Phase 1 — Interactive 3D roof measurement/reporting

- Property-linked full-width 3D house/roof editor and plan view: orbit/zoom/touch, planes/vertices/dimensions, undo/redo, multiple structures, save/reopen; gable/hip/shed/flat/mansard/custom complex shapes.
- Calculate sloped area, squares, pitch, perimeter and ridge/hip/valley/eave/rake totals; shared edges count once. Correct classifications and flag invalid/overlapping geometry. Add chimneys/skylights/vents/penetrations; waste stays separate.
- Condition, existing/recommended system, inspection notes and measured-versus-estimated status. Private report/diagram/aerial/drone/before-after uploads; calibrated image tracing and reviewed AI import. Address/unscaled image alone cannot produce verified dimensions.
- Versioned drawings and branded shareable PDFs with drawings/dimensions/edges/pitch/penetrations/quantities/photos. Reviewed snapshots transfer to material takeoff and estimating without re-entry or historical overwrites.

### Phase 2 — Intelligent estimator and roofing calculators

- Editable quantity/price/labor catalogs and templates for every requested system: shingle/tile/metal variants, flat/TPO/modified/built-up/coatings, gutters/fascia/soffit/repairs/waterproofing and custom systems.
- All requested material/accessory/labor/equipment/disposal/permit/other costs; plane-level panel counts/lengths with configurable coverage/overlap/package/waste assumptions. Missing prices stay unset; installed instant rates remain separate.
- Integer-cent costs/overhead, markup versus margin, discounts/taxes, final prices/deposits and reconciled payment schedules. Customer-comparison options reference one property/drawing revision; costs/profit remain private. Approval generates linked proposal/contract drafts.
- Consolidate requested pitch/slope/squares/waste/bundle/panel/edge/underlayment/gutter/labor/profit/tax calculators. Project-specific Florida Building Code/HVHZ settings require manual verification, not automatic compliance claims.

### Phase 3 — Documents, templates and signatures

- Extend PDF tools with a drag/drop structured composer for text, logos/contact details, images/tables/pricing, custom sections/terms/dates and signature/initial fields.
- All requested document types: estimates/proposals/contracts, roof/inspection reports, work/material/change orders, invoices/requests/receipts, completion/warranty documents and customer letters; email/follow-up templates.
- Upload/create/edit/duplicate/categorize/delete templates; autofill linked customer/property/project/price/deposit/balance/date/company variables. Branded PDF export/print; drafts now, actual emails when connected.
- Preserve originals and immutable revisions. Edit supported fields; scanned/flattened PDFs use identified overlays or composer rebuilding. No arbitrary original-text replacement, secure redaction or full Acrobat-parity promise.
- Version-bound approvals/signatures/initials/terms/timestamps/audit history; compliant signature service only when authorized. Basic sign-offs do not claim certified legal compliance.

### Phase 4 — Projects and production

- Expand Jobs with dates/system/contract value/manager/assignments, budget versus actual costs, daily reports/tasks, private photos/videos/drone footage, internal comments and approved customer updates.
- Fourteen stages: Approved → Contract Signed → Deposit Received → Materials Ordered → Scheduled → Tear-Off → Deck Inspection → Underlayment → Roofing Installation → Flashing → Final Inspection → Punch List → Completed → Final Payment; custom stages cannot bypass evidence.
- Linked day/week/month calendar for requested appointment/project/team/inspection/delivery/follow-up/payment events; record deliveries and manual weather delays.
- Approved estimate creates/updates one project; correct signed contract plus recorded required deposit makes it ready for scheduling. Repeated events cannot duplicate projects. Field milestones are explicitly recorded.
- Change orders update linked scope/budget/schedule revisions and new invoice drafts, never issued amounts. Completion creates reports/final invoice drafts; owner issues invoices. Portal receives approved progress only.

### Phase 5 — Actual financial analytics

- Recorded expenses/receipts and allocations for every requested cost category, with project/system/employee/salesperson/date attribution.
- Separate signed value, issued sales and collected revenue; outstanding includes all current issued balances. Named cash-based gross/net profit/margins use actual receipts/costs; incomplete costs are flagged, forecasts separate and adjustments traceable.
- Requested revenue/approval/conversion/completion/source metrics, date/team/project/system filters, cross-session refresh, PDF and genuine Excel exports. Exclude marked demo records and sandbox transactions from live totals; failed reads never become trustworthy zeros.

## Connected operational extensions

- **Team:** employee invitations/records, schedules/time/labor/performance and all requested owner/office/sales/estimator/project/production/field/accounting permissions, enforced server-side.
- **Inventory/suppliers:** stock/usage ledger, reorder alerts, supplier history, purchase orders/deliveries linked to estimating; generation does not imply supplier submission.
- **Billing:** progress/final invoices, discounts/partials, deposit/material/progress/final/custom requests bounded by actual balances, receipts and event-based sent/viewed statuses. Settlement alone confirms payment; no stored card data or silent issued-amount changes.
- **Portal:** option approvals, signatures/initials, approved updates/photos, payments/receipts/downloads/messages, strictly customer-isolated with no internal costs.
- **Files:** private searchable library by customer/property/project/category/date; PDF/image/HEIC/video/Excel/Word uploads, supported previews/downloads. Convert HEIC for display while preserving originals.
- **Automations:** all requested reminders/notifications, configurable triggers/templates; in-app first, email/SMS after authorization, with duplicate/delivery audit.
- **Website/social:** secure photo/source-aware lead intake; verify outside-site access first. Authorized Google Business Profile/Facebook/Instagram APIs only where supported.
- **Security/settings:** validation/audit/error handling and role-aware files; verify backups instead of assuming they are active.

These attach to the same phase-specific records, not separate applications. Follow all 25 requested sidebar destinations, grouped for mobile; relocate working screens and expose new destinations only when operational.

## Technical safeguards and dependencies

Enforce server-side team access, private files and narrow customer projections. Independently test geometry/money rules; Three.js loads browser-only. ## Completion gate

Each phase requires rule/permission tests, signed-in owner/customer flows, desktop/mobile checks, error inspection and QA cleanup. Report delivery and blockers before advancing. Begin Phase 0, then prioritize 3D → materials → estimates while preserving invoicing/payments.
